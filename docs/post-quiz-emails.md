# Post-quiz follow-up emails (GHL workflow "Quiz Follow-Up Sequence", 6 emails)

Trigger: tag `quiz-completed` added (the quiz webhook workflow adds it).
Stops early if the contact books a private strategy call. After the last email, they move to the weekly emails.
Sender: Rola Diab <rola@roladiab.com>. Replies forward to rola@alorsolutions.com.
The instant results email still comes from the quiz itself, so this starts on day 1.

**Email rules (adapted from the Ultimate Sales Machine email of Nov 6, 2026):**
1. The preview line keeps the subject's story going. Never leave it blank.
2. Open inside a moment: a scene, a line someone said, a real result. No warm-up.
3. Give proof with a source: a named person, a real client result, or a study.
4. Ask one question they answer in their head, ideally with simple math.
5. Longer emails use short bold subheads so a skimmer still gets the point.
6. One ask in the body, and keep it small (often a one-word reply).
7. End with the same branded sign-off every time, then the booking line.
8. Optional P.S. that gives something extra, never a second pitch.
9. Lots of white space: one idea per paragraph, one to two lines each, nothing longer than three lines on a phone.
10. Play the long game. Only a small share of any list is ready right now, so keep showing up weekly.

**Every email ends with this line in the signature block (links to the questionnaire). Approved 2026-11-07. If the email body already uses Conviction Code™, drop the ™ here (one ™ per email):**
When you're ready to create inevitable success, the Conviction Code™ clears what's in the way, from the decisions you can see to the beliefs you can't. Book a private strategy call: https://quiz.convictioncode.com/apply/
